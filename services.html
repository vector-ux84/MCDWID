Service Applications

The district serves the Heritage District, New Saddleback Vista, and Valle Escondido neighborhoods. Subdivisions and housing developments (Rancho El Dorado, Villages, Tortosa, Senita, etc.) are not serviced — contact Global Water at 866-940-1102.

Application 1

New Service

Requires a copy of photo ID and renter or owner documents, plus the service deposit. Print the application, then drop it off with the deposit at the office (45290 W. Garvey Ave.) or email it in. Application and deposit must both be completed before service can be connected; turn-on is usually the next day.

New Service Application (PDF) →

Application 2

Construction / Development

Requires a copy of photo ID, a point of contact for the project, a map of the project area, and the project's start and projected completion dates, plus the meter deposit. Submit at the office or by email; application and deposit must both be completed before service can be connected.

Construction Meter Agreement (PDF) → Development & Construction Permit Application (PDF) →

Application 3

Hydrant Service

Requires a copy of photo ID, a point of contact for the project, a map of the project area, and start/completion dates, plus a deposit. Once submitted and processed, you'll receive an account number; turn-on is usually the next day.
