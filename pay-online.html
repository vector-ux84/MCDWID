About this payment portal

Maricopa Consolidated Domestic Water Improvement District has partnered with NexBillPay to process all online water bill payments. NexBillPay is our only approved online payment processor, operating a secure portal separate from this website.

When you continue, you will be directed to NexBillPay's site (new.nexbillpay.net) to view your account balance, payment history, and submit a payment. This site is not operated by the district; please review the address bar to confirm you're on new.nexbillpay.net before entering any payment information.

If you receive a request to pay your water bill through any other website, app, or service, please contact our office before making a payment.
