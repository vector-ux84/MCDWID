Service area

Is your address in our service area?

The district serves three areas: the Heritage District (the original community area of Maricopa), New Saddleback Vista (merged 2017), and Valle Escondido (merged 2020). Use the map or the address lookup tool below to confirm we're your water provider before requesting new service. The district does not currently serve subdivisions or housing developments (Rancho El Dorado, Villages, Tortosa, Senita, etc.) — residents there should contact Global Water at 866-940-1102.
