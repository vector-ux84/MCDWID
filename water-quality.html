Nitrate notice

Nitrate Mitigation Guidance

Water filtration is currently the only effective method for removing nitrates from drinking water. Residents seeking immediate mitigation are encouraged to consider a reverse osmosis system; home filtration units are generally available in the $75–$500 range, depending on capacity and configuration. The District remains committed to keeping customers informed as system-level nitrate reduction improvements are planned and implemented.

Water quality reports covering the past three years are available at the district office (45290 W. Garvey Ave.). Prior-year reports are no longer distributed by mail or email.
