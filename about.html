Where we came from

District history

July 21, 1986 — District formed The Pinal County Board of Supervisors formed the district to acquire the area's sole private water utility.
February 1998 — Designated a Colonia A federal underserved-community designation, often tied to infrastructure funding eligibility.
November 2017 — Merged with New Saddleback Vista DWID This merger is where "Consolidated" entered the district's name.
November 2020 — Valle Escondido DWID merged in Merger followed a district election.
